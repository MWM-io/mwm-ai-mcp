# Example prompts

[Install](installation.md) · [Capabilities](capabilities.md)

Replace the app, country, locale, dates, and audience with your own details.
Give your assistant the intended outcome and say when you want drafts only.

## Connect and check access

> Help me connect MWM AI, verify its tools with a publishing knowledge lookup,
> and show me how to connect my store accounts.

## Research a market

> I am building a habit tracker for US iPhone users. Find relevant competitors,
> compare their positioning and keyword opportunities, and explain the dates
> and coverage of the data you use.

## Improve a store listing

> Audit my app's English App Store listing. Explain the main gaps, then draft
> a subtitle, keyword field, and description. Keep everything as drafts.

## Create visuals in the app's style

> Read my app's brand guide and prepare an icon direction and a five-screen
> screenshot storyboard. Show the brief and any expected generation cost before
> generating the assets.

## Create an audience-specific page

> Prepare an App Store custom product page for people searching for beginner
> meditation. Reuse the app's approved brand direction and keep the page as a
> draft for review.

## Understand reviews

> Analyze my latest App Store version's reviews in France. Group the complaints,
> compare them with the previous version, and draft replies to the most useful
> reviews for me to review.

## Compare app performance

> Compare my app's last complete month's revenue and retention with the month
> before. Explain the metric definitions, cohort windows, and missing data.

## Plan a store event

> Draft an App Store in-app event for our upcoming drawing challenge, including
> the creative brief and localized text. Check current store requirements and
> cite their sources. Do not submit it.

## Prepare a release

> Check my app's current App Store release status and what is missing for the
> next submission. Show me a reviewable plan before making changes.

## Prepare privacy information

> Help me identify the data collection in my app's code and SDKs, then prepare
> App Privacy answers for review. Explain which steps need my browser or a store
> account connection.

If a task needs a missing account connection, app identifier, role, or client
capability, follow the setup guidance returned by MWM. See
[troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) when no actual MWM tool result appears.
